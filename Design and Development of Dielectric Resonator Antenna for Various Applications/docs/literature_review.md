# Literature review summary

Twenty DRA papers were reviewed in two stages. Reference numbers match the paper.

## Stage 1: fundamentals and enhancement techniques

| Ref | Authors (year) | Topic | Takeaway |
|---|---|---|---|
| [1] | Mukherjee, Patel, Mukherjee (2020) | DRA review: modes, feeds, geometries, CP, MIMO, arrays | Survey baseline |
| [2] | Moni et al. (2016) | Cylindrical ZST DRA, εr 15 to 45 | Higher εr: resonance 3.74 → 2.99 GHz, bandwidth 160 → 70 MHz (size-bandwidth trade-off) |
| [3] | Dave, Trivedi, Patel (2018) | Rectangular DRA for 5G | Simple RDRA serves 8.3 and 11.6 GHz |
| [4] | Ali et al. (2019) | Perforated DRA, higher-order mode | 9.3 dBi, 96 % efficiency at 26 GHz |
| [5] | Ali et al. (2020) | Hollow DRA | 16.1 % measured bandwidth at 15 GHz |
| [6] | Al-Alem, Kishk, Antar (2020) | Dielectric ring around a 60 GHz DRA | Higher boresight gain, better pattern symmetry |
| [7] | Zhang et al. (2024) | DRA technology for 5G/6G | LTCC/PCB integration, beam-steering arrays |
| [8] | Srivastava et al. (2025) | Stacked alumina CP DRA + ML | 111.1 % bandwidth, 12.8 dBic, ML performance prediction |

| Ref | Authors (year) | Resonator | Notes |

| [9] | Surendra, Khan (2020) | Alumina CDRA Ø20 × 10, εr 9.9 | 4.16 GHz, 7.36 dBi directivity |
| [10] | Goutia, Tiwari (2021) | CDRA | 4 GHz, 6.8 dBi, 3.4-5 GHz |

| [13] | Prasanth, Guruviah (2017) | Partially grounded CDRA | Multiband 2-6 GHz |

| [15] | Ashaari et al. (2018) | Rectangular DRA | Dual band, 7.57 dB at 7 GHz |

| [17] | Patel, Upadhyaya (2022) | Four-port MIMO DRA | 5.8/6.2 dBi, ~90 % efficiency |
| [18] | Pan, Li, Zhang (2023) | Al₂O₃ CP DRA array | 53.2 % bandwidth, 12.48 dBic |
| [19] | Trinh-Van et al. (2016) | GA-shaped pixelated alumina DRA | 6.13 dBic |
| [20] | Omar et al. (2022) | Al₂O₃-SiO₂-MgZrO₃ stacked half-cylinders | 61.01 % bandwidth, 11.96-22.46 GHz |

Alumina is the resonator in 12 of the 20 papers. Zirconium appears only inside compounds ([2] ZST, [20] MgZrO₃).

## Zirconia outside this set

[21] thin flexible zirconia antenna substrate (εr ≈ 22); [22] 3D-printed DRAs for space; [23] 3D-printed 2.45 GHz MIMO DRA (εr 23, tan δ 0.0015); [24] manufacturer property chart; [25] Balanis, *Antenna Theory*.

Full reference list with DOIs: see `docs/paper/Zirconia_DRA_for_5_8_GHz.docx`.

## Research gap

No simple microstrip-fed solid zirconia CDRA for 5.8 GHz compared directly with alumina on the same board and feed was found.
